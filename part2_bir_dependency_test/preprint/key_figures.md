## Figure 3 — panel specification

The paper's new centre of gravity (`draft_outline.md` §3). All three panels are
deterministic: no p-value, gate or verdict is required to read them.

**3a — three genes, three dependency regimes.** Strip or dot plot of Chronos gene effect
across the 16 ALT+ lines for POLD3, RAD52, SMARCAL1, with reference lines at 0 and −1.0
(≈ median common essential) and the −0.5 dependency threshold marked.
Source: `autoresearch/iteration_03_bir_module_coherence/data/h4_marginals.csv`.
Key values: POLD3 median −1.666, 16/16 below −0.5; RAD52 median −0.010, 0/16;
SMARCAL1 median −0.462, 8/16 below −0.5 and 3/16 below −1.0.

**3b — the composite measured its own comparator.** Horizontal bar of each gene's share of
the composite's variance, BIR arm and HR arm distinguished, zero line marked so RAD52's
negative contribution is visible.
Source: `autoresearch/iteration_03_bir_module_coherence/data/h4_variance_decomposition.csv`.
Key values: BRCA2 25.8%, SMARCAL1 23.4%, BRCA1 21.9%, RAD51 20.5%, POLD3 11.1%,
**RAD52 −2.7%**; HR arm 68.2% versus BIR arm 31.8%. The decomposition is exact — note in
the caption that the identity holds on unrounded values.

**3c — a module, a non-module, and the calibration.** Forest of mean pairwise Spearman T
per gene set with its permutation p and its pre-registered α = 0.05 detection floor drawn
per set (floors differ with k).
Source: `autoresearch/iteration_03_bir_module_coherence/data/h4_modules.csv`.
Rows to show, in this order:

```
BTR (BLM/TOP3A/RMI1/RMI2)      k=4  T +0.5255  p 0.0001  floor 0.2029  clears
HR (BRCA1/BRCA2/RAD51)         k=3  T +0.4137  p 0.0039  floor 0.2892  clears
BIR triplet (POLD3/RAD52/SMARCAL1) k=3  T -0.1824  p 0.2285  floor 0.2892  does not clear
Batenburg BIR (MUS81/RAD52/POLD3)  k=3  T -0.2735  p 0.0665  floor 0.2892  does not clear
```

Caption must carry the calibration argument explicitly: BTR clears at n = 16 **while
containing one near-saturated member (TOP3A, 16/16 below −0.5) and two members essential in
no line (BLM 0/16, RMI2 0/16)**, so "16 lines is too few" is not available as an
explanation for the BIR triplet's result.
