# Abstract draft

**~150 words. Draft for the author to revise; every number traces to a committed artifact.**

---

Alternative lengthening of telomeres (ALT) sustains a large fraction of osteosarcomas and
depends on break-induced replication (BIR). Structural variants at 17p13.1 inactivate the
telomerase chaperone TCAB1 together with WRAP53β, predicting that affected cells lean
harder on BIR. We pre-registered that prediction and tested it in 16 ALT+ DepMap
osteosarcoma lines: Cliff's δ = +0.281 (95% CI −0.313 to +0.844), p = 0.382, opposite in
sign to the prediction, minimum BH q = 0.959. Four further pre-registered iterations, each
frozen before its outcome was read, recovered no association (campaign-wide q = 0.420,
0.420, 1.000). The composite endpoint proves unusable in this cohort: two of its three BIR
genes carry no dependency variation, and 68.2% of its variance comes from the comparator
arm, which — unlike the BIR arm — is a coherent module. Co-essentiality is nonetheless
detectable at n = 16, so these nulls are informative within stated floors rather than
underpowered.
