# Iteration 01 outcome — H1: WRAP53 expression vs ALT status

**Verdict: `counter_signal`.** The estimate runs opposite to the prediction and
stays there under every robustness check, but does not reach significance.

Pre-specification `prespec.md` v1.0.1, frozen `8e6ed51`, erratum `2d68239`,
both before any exposure–outcome join. Notebook idempotent: re-running
reproduces `notebook_01_sha256.json` byte-for-byte.

---

## Result

| | n | δ (ALT+ vs ALT−) | 95% CI | p | AUROC | MDE \|δ\| |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** (patient-collapsed) | 18 v 12 | **+0.241** | −0.185 to +0.667 | **0.280** | 0.380 | 0.435 |

The prediction was δ **< 0** (ALT+ lines lower in WRAP53). Observed: ALT+ lines
are **higher** — median 4.193 vs 3.483 log2(TPM+1), Hodges-Lehmann shift
+0.388. AUROC for "low WRAP53 ⇒ ALT+" is 0.380, i.e. mildly *anti*-predictive.

## The two known-information guards strengthen the counter-signal

This is the most consequential thing in the iteration. `DISCLOSURES.md` recorded
4 of 31 label–exposure pairs as known before the freeze, and both guards were
pre-specified to drop them.

| | n | δ | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | 18 v 12 | +0.241 | 0.280 |
| **R3** — drop the two WRAP53-zero ALT+ lines | 16 v 12 | **+0.396** | 0.082 |
| **R3b** — drop all four known pairs | 15 v 11 | **+0.418** | 0.077 |

The two WRAP53 = 0 ALT+ lines (OS384, NRH-OS4) were the *only* feature pulling
toward H1. Removing them nearly doubles the counter-directional effect. Per §3
the guard would have become the primary interpretation had it disagreed in
direction; it agrees, and reinforces.

So the one piece of pre-existing evidence that looked like support for H1 —
"the two lines with zero WRAP53 are both ALT+" — is, in the full cohort, two
outliers inside a distribution that trends the other way.

## Robustness: the direction does not move

- **R6 leave-one-line-out:** 30 of 30 refits positive, δ ∈ [+0.192, +0.343].
  No single line drives it.
- **R8 leave-one-depositor-out:** 12 of 12 refits positive, δ ∈ [+0.086,
  +0.358]. **The depositor confound does not explain the result.** The weakest
  refit drops Montefiore Einstein (0 ALT+ : 3 ALT−), the block most capable of
  manufacturing this direction, and the effect survives at δ = +0.086.
- **R1 gold/high-confidence labels only** (12 v 7): δ = +0.286, p = 0.340. Same
  direction; silver labels are not responsible.
- **R2 Stage 1's 25 CRISPR-screened lines** (16 v 9): δ = +0.069, p = 0.799 —
  same direction, much attenuated. The cohort expansion did not create the
  effect; it made a pre-existing one visible.
- **R7 uncollapsed** (18 v 13): δ = +0.171, p = 0.435. Same direction.

## Two findings that were pre-registered as descriptive, and matter

**R4 — copy number runs the other way.** WRAP53 gene-level CN gives δ = −0.167
(p = 0.465, AUROC 0.583): *mildly in H1's predicted direction*, opposite to the
mRNA. The two measurement layers of the same gene disagree in sign. Neither is
significant, so this is a discordance rather than a result — but it points at
the mRNA layer as the failing component, which is exactly what the literature
scan predicted (Keegan's own RNA-seq found normal TCAB1 transcript in
SV-disrupted samples; Jiang 2025 puts the control at the CCT/TRiC protein
layer).

**R5 — TP53 expression discriminates, and WRAP53 does not.** TP53
log2(TPM+1) gives δ = **−0.472, p = 0.031**, AUROC 0.736: ALT+ lines have
**lower TP53**, significantly, in the direction H1 predicted for its own gene.

This was pre-specified as **descriptive and non-gating** (§7), it is one of
eight robustness contrasts, and it has not faced campaign FDR. It is not a
result of this iteration and is not treated as one. But it inverts the §7
expectation in an informative way: the prespec said a co-deletion architecture
would predict a *same-direction, weaker* TP53 effect, and a WRAP53-specific
effect a stronger WRAP53 one. What happened is the reverse — the 17p13.1 locus
does appear to carry ALT-status information in this cohort, and it is carried by
**TP53**, not WRAP53.

That is consistent with Keegan et al.'s own reading: the SVs land in TP53
intron 1 and inactivate TP53 completely, clipping the 1,419-nt TCAB1 overlap
only incidentally — "loss of TCAB1 may be a consequence of TP53 SVs." If the
SV's primary target is TP53, TP53 is where an expression signature should show.
**This is the strongest hypothesis generated by iteration 01 and is carried to
the candidate list, not acted on here.**

## What this does and does not establish

**Does not support H1 in its mRNA form.** The estimate is counter-directional,
stable across 42 refits, and strengthens when the known pairs are removed.

**Does not reach significance, and the memo will not pretend otherwise.**
|δ| = 0.241 is below the 0.435 detection floor; p = 0.280. §8 pre-registered
that a counter-directional result would be interpretable *at* |δ| ≥ 0.435, and
this is not that. R3b comes closest (|δ| = 0.418, p = 0.077) and still falls
short. **Suggestive, not established.**

**Does not touch Keegan's tumour-level SV claim.** That claim is about
structural variants in tumour WGS, assayed by IHC at the protein level. Nothing
here bears on it. What iteration 01 shows is narrower and more specific: in
DepMap 26Q1 osteosarcoma *cell lines*, WRAP53 **mRNA** does not carry the lesion,
and if anything trends the wrong way.

**Sharpens Stage 1's central limitation with data rather than citation.**
Stage 1 limitation #2 said WRAP53 expression is a proxy for 17p13.1 disruption,
not a measurement of it. Iteration 01 adds: the proxy is anti-correlated with
ALT status (AUROC 0.380), its own copy number disagrees in sign, and the
neighbouring gene discriminates significantly while it does not. **Stage 1's
verdict is unchanged** — this explains it, it does not revise it, and no Stage 1
artefact was modified.

## Limitations

1. **Underpowered by design.** MDE 0.435 at 18v12; 27% power at Keegan's own
   35% prevalence under perfect separation. Recorded in §9 before the result.
2. **Batch confounding remains formally unfalsifiable** — no profiling metadata
   exists in this repo (§11.2). R8 is reassuring but is a depositor proxy, not
   a sequencing-batch control.
3. **All 31 labels trace to one upstream source** (ALTitude 2024); tier is a
   perfect function of provenance, so R1 tests label tier, not independence.
4. **The authoritative ALT tier definitions do not exist in this repo** —
   `label_spec_v1.0.md` §2 is a placeholder and v0.9 is absent (§11.5).
5. **Cell lines are not tumours**, and Mahmoudi 2011's finding that WRAP53 is
   typically overexpressed in cancer lines — with U2OS and Saos-2 named — means
   the panel may be survivorship-selected against genuinely WRAP53-low states.
   The counter-direction observed here is what that selection predicts.
6. 4 of 31 label–exposure pairs were known before the freeze (`DISCLOSURES.md`).
   R3/R3b quantify the consequence rather than removing it.
